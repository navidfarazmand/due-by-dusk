# Due by Dusk mobile architecture (v0.12)

Landscape farm game: Gemini's v0.6.05 farm engine runs the harvest days; Claude's game shell runs everything around them. TypeScript + Vite + Capacitor 8. Android and iOS hold local web assets; no server is needed to play.

## Pieces

- `src/engine/gemini-engine.js`: Gemini's v0.6.05 bundle (Phaser shim, crops, characters, sounds, effects), formatted and patched. It draws the field on `#field`, keeps its own farm save (`dbd-farm-v3`) and exposes `window.__DBD_ENGINE` (state, bills, start day, pay, perks, tools, boss info). The shell talks to it only through that API and the `window.__dbdEvent` events (`dayStart`, `dayEnd`, `idle`, `billPaid`, `perks`, `cityWon`, `foreclosed`, `bossAppear`, `bossStomp`, `bossDown`). Gemini's developer studios and Arthur are removed. Its old `__DBD_TEST` hook exists only in dev and e2e builds.
- `src/game/`: pure game rules, no DOM, unit-tested.
  - `world.ts`: 7 countries, 30 cities in route order, trios, rivals. The last city of each country is its boss farm.
  - `plan.ts`: what each farm asks (`planFor`): five to eight bills, two days apart; the crop ladder (carrots first, one new crop per bill paid, each with its own value and toughness via `cropVal` and `cropHp`); crop health and value per city, field density, content unlocks, the city record, the spare-cash goal for the second star, the boss.
  - `progress.ts`: the game save (`dbd-game-v3`): wins, stars, crowns, wallet, workshop, tools, skills, ribbons, looks, today's daily farm, ad counters, settings; country and city locks; stars. Older saves get ribbons for the stars, crowns and bosses they already have.
  - `workshop.ts`: permanent upgrades in small steps (`SHED_FX`, read by the engine as `window.__DBD_SHED_FX`); prices rise about 11% a level; every fifth level is a timed job (one at a time). `oldWorkshopRefund` refunds v0.9 levels when an older save loads.
  - `tools.ts`: sickle, scythe, flame weeder, seed blaster and their attachments; `toolStats` becomes `window.__DBD_TOOL` for the engine.
  - `power.ts`: `fieldPower`, a build's strength when a farm starts (damage per second on one crop, crits averaged in, times the swing's area or the seeds per shot). `toughnessFor` in `plan.ts` turns it into the run's `tough`: crops are tougher by the square root of the build's lead over `expectedPower(k)` (softer for a build behind it). The shell gives the engine `farmPlan(run)`, the city plan with `hp × tough`; side farms keep the plain plan.
  - `skills.ts`: the skill map. 19 of the engine's own skills (`hasSkill`) in four branches, bought with diamonds (one per farm won, two per boss farm, one per daily gold medal). Nodes stay in the clouds until a neighbour is learned; reset is free. The whole route earns exactly what every skill costs.
  - `flair.ts`: the Bling Barn, looks only (tool finish, swing trail, bird, coins), bought with ribbons. `lookFor` becomes `window.__DBD_LOOK`, which the engine reads when it draws.
  - `daily.ts`: the daily farm and duels. One-day side farms on a seeded field with a set loadout, medal targets, and the day's tries.
- `src/shell/`: the screens (menu, world and country maps, farmhouse, sundown, won and lost, modals; `extras.ts` for the skill map, Bling Barn, daily farm and duel), the farm HUD, rewarded offers, music (eight tracks, one picked for the field and one for the menus), the item and skill drawings (`looks.ts`), art lookup and layout scaling.
  - `coach.ts`: Level 0 (the practice farm), the intro and the lessons. Since v0.11.1 a new player's Play opens the practice farm: the first farm's carrots with the daylight clock stopped (`setClock`), a step card at the top (cut six carrots, the jar pays the bills, a chain of six) that waits for the player, then the sun runs out (`setDayLeft`) and the first real farm opens; Skip practice (and the pause card) leave at once. Settings replays the practice farm and the intro film. On real farms, the first sight of something new (the boss, a radish, a golden crop, the goat, a crow, a prop, lightning, the rival, the chain, a rush) holds the field (`hold`) with a card until a tap; nothing dims the screen. Older notes: It drives the real field (a hand that touches through pointer events, slow motion, spotlights, a word or two) instead of rule screens: the intro farm's boss falls about ten seconds before sunset, then each mechanic (cut, chain, the power, Storm, rival, diamonds, ribbons) is shown the first time it matters. `seen` flags in the save keep each lesson to once; `window.__DBD_NO_COACH` turns it off for tests.
  - `rival.ts`: the face drawn for each city's rival (the HUD chip that shows how close today is to their best day, the won card).
  - Volume: Settings keeps a music and a sound-effects volume (0–100 %, squared to a gain). `music.ts` multiplies its track levels by the music gain and the dawn rooster by the effects gain; the engine's effects go through `setSfxVolume` (master gain 2.1 × the effects gain, then a soft limiter). v0.11 raised the effects by about 20 dB against the music: they had been about 15 dB under it on a phone speaker.
- `src/i18n/`: five languages. `t('English text', {vars})` and `tn(n, one, many)` look the English text up in `fr.ts`, `es.ts`, `pt.ts` or `de.ts` (English is the key, so untranslated text shows in English); `tx` translates the words the engine draws on the field (`window.__DBD_T`). The language is the player's pick (`settings.lang`), else the phone's. `npm run i18n:check` lists any text without a translation.
- `src/platform/`: native storage mirror, lifecycle, orientation, haptics and AdMob (rewarded ads only, with Google's consent message).

## Rules worth keeping

- Money (v0.12.2, Navid 5 Nov: option B). The farm's cash (in the engine) pays its bills and buys upgrades and tools during a run. What is left after the deed is banked as savings (`g.wallet`, `bankAfterWin`): savings buy upgrades and tools, never a bill, and only cash above the farm's mark is banked (a farm pays out once), so an easy farm can't be replayed for upgrade money. In 0.13 savings become the upgrade tree's credit. Free-coin ads pay into the farm in progress. A foreclosure keeps savings, workshop levels and tools.
- A farm pays out once (`paid` per city, `mark` and `spent` per run): during a run only farm cash above the farm's `paid` buys upgrades, and only cash above it after the deed is banked. Quitting or losing still adds what the farm's cash bought. So replays, retries and quitting early can't mine a farm twice (Navid, 29 Oct).
- A bill is paid on the evening it is due, never earlier, so every farm runs its full length. Bills give 2, 3, 4, then 5 days (`billDays`): a five-bill farm is 19 days, Japan's boss farm 34. A new crop joins every morning (`stageOnDay`: day 1 carrots, day 2 the radish, then the country's three and the staples; the engine's `unlockLevel` is the farm's unlock plus the day), and the day card names it. Days are 15 s with no upgrades (`DAY_SECONDS`); Longer days and skills add up to about 15 s.
- Perks come in a chest after every bill but the deed: wooden (+5%), silver (+10% and one-per-farm tricks), gold (+15%, Heavy swing, Storm, Cleaver). The kind is rolled (`CHEST_ODDS` in the engine: 70/22/8 % for the first two chests, 30/50/20 % after; the chest before the deed is always gold). One rewarded ad per chest upgrades it while it is closed, at least one kind and sometimes to diamond (four gold perks, pick two). Perks last for that farm.
- Touch: the sickle sits above the finger by the finger offset (Settings → Finger offset, 0–80 CSS px, 40 by default; Off cuts exactly under the finger) so the finger does not hide the crops it aims at, fading out near the bottom edge so the bottom row stays reachable. The coach's ghost finger touches below its target by the same offset (`fingerAt`), and the practice farm's first step says the sickle cuts just above the fingertip. A second finger does nothing. (v0.11.1-0.11.4 had a hold-still Charge Cleave; v0.12 replaced it with the power meter, below.)
- Field (v0.11.3): crops are drawn 25% bigger than in v0.11.0 (Navid liked the size) and there are as many as the upgrades allow; Richer soil adds ¾ of a crop a level (½ before; Navid: "a level 9 upgrade gives 7 more crops instead of 5"), and crops spawn one at a time as before. v0.11.1-0.11.2 doubled the field and added bunches; that was too many crops and made phones stutter (a 30-crop field ran at 11 frames a second where 15 crops ran at 16, on a 4x slowed CPU), and bunches broke the chain between them. A bot that chases the nearest ripe crop all day earns what it did in v0.11.0 (Charlottetown about $58, Austin about $340). Engine knobs: `FIELD` (size, count, spawn, pay, chain), overridable with `window.__DBD_FIELD`.
- Smoothness (v0.11.3): almost all of a frame is filling the canvas's pixels, so the field starts at up to 2 canvas pixels per screen point and, during a day, steps down in proportion (to 1.25 at the least) when the middle of 60 frames takes longer than 21 ms; the setting is kept for the next launch (`dbd-dpr-cap`). On a 4x slowed CPU: 14 frames a second at 2, 21 at 1.5, 28 at 1.25. `window.__DBD_DPR_CAP` pins it for tests. The snow draws as a few batched shapes (settled flakes, falling flakes, puffs, ice rings) instead of one shape each.
- Weather by city (v0.12; `src/game/citylook.ts`, one row a city, a city without one takes its country's): snow (falling flakes, a white field that deepens towards dusk, ice around every crop, a puff of snow at each cut), autumn leaves (Gatineau: leaves drift down, lie on the soil and fly up from a cut) or a clear field; the engine reads the table from `window.__DBD_WX` (`countryWx`, `updateWeather`, `drawSnowGround`, `drawSnowFront`; `window.__DBD_NO_WEATHER` turns it off for performance tests). A city's landmark is in its own skyline band (`src/art/bands/<city id>.webp`, picked up by name, else the country's band). Planned for the other countries: USA dry wind and dust, Brazil rain and mud, France autumn leaves, Morocco heat shimmer and sand, China drizzle, Japan cherry blossom.
- Menu (v0.11.1, Navid picked Design 4 of five): a signpost. Play is the big board and goes straight to the day card of the farm in progress (or starts the next farm); the plank above it is the next farm and opens the world map; arrow signs lead to the daily farm, duels and the Barn (the skill map or the Bling Barn). Language, music and sound are in Settings (the gear). Crowns live on the world map, whose crown count explains them.
- Pause card: Music and Sound effects switches, Back to the field, Back to menu. Leaving a day puts the farm back at dawn (`abandonDay`, as closing the app does); a daily farm or duel turn ends with what it earned. The field's sound button opens it; the day's event badge holds the field (`hold`) until the next tap.
- The free-coins ad pays about one ordinary day of the player's own (median of the last five route days, `coinsForDays`). Short on tonight's bill, a late day (an ad) is the big button: up to three late days a bill (`lateDay` in the engine), and at each late day's sunset Grumble & Sons take 20%, then 40%, then 60% of what it earned (`LATE_FEES`; Sundown shows the fee). The bill itself stays the same; after the third late day only the bank is left. Paying the bill clears the count.
- Android: strictly landscape (`sensorLandscape` in the manifest and a runtime lock), fullscreen immersive, drawn under the system bars (`setDecorFitsSystemWindows(false)`), into the camera cutout (`ALWAYS` from Android 11, `SHORT_EDGES` before). v0.11.1–0.11.2 tried Play Console's large-screen and edge-to-edge suggestions; `EdgeToEdge.enable()` before `super.onCreate` put an old-style title bar across the top of the game, and the free rotation added a "turn sideways" screen Navid did not want, so v0.11.3 went back to this. The manifest's `appCategory="game"` keeps Android 16 from overriding the landscape lock on large screens. The field keeps crops at least 3.5% of its width (or the camera cutout) away from each side.
- A boss farm's deed cannot be paid until its boss is knocked out. Boss health is set when the farm starts from the player's field power, the farm's days and how much of the day its armour is off (`share`, `uptime`), and it carries over between days. v0.12 (Navid, 5 Nov): the boss walks to the most valuable crop near it and eats it (`bossEat`; the coins go into its belly); its armour is off for 3.2 s after a meal (`BOSS_FULL`), and only then do hits hurt it and spit a quarter of the meal back as coins; armoured hits clink. From its second phase it throws bomb seeds (cutting one costs 5% of the cash and stuns the sickle for a second); from its third it calls the country's storm (ice in Canada and Japan, sand in Morocco and the USA, mud elsewhere) that encases five crops for two hits each, with no bonus for freeing them. It can fall mid-farm only if the player spends the days on it instead of the crops.
- Thief crows (v0.12, every farm): a crow flies to the jar, takes 6% of the cash and flies low across the field to leave; a swipe before it is gone gets the coins back (`crowSteal`, `crowBack`, `crowAway` events).
- The power meter (v0.12, Navid 5 Nov; it replaced the hold-still Charge Cleave and the RUSH counter): beside the sun dial. Each cut crop adds its chain multiplier (`powerGain`: ×1.25 a Power booster level, ×1.5 with Power mastery, ×2 on the windy day); at 22 it fires the tool's power where the finger is (or on the thickest crops) together with the Harvest Rush (+50% pay, a faster field, 4 s). Sickle: Cleave, one big cut around it. Scythe: Whirlwind, a vortex that follows the finger for about 2.5 s. Flame weeder: Fire wave, a ring of fire that sets every crop it passes alight. Seed blaster: Seed storm, a crit seed at each of the 12 nearest crops. The Power booster attachment (it was the Charge handle, same id) is on every tool and also makes the power hit harder. A card explains the meter once per tool, at half full.
- Arthur Grumble (v0.12; `src/shell/arthur-art.js` draws him, `arthur.ts` gives him moods) stands on the HUD left of the sound button: neutral by default, gloating on a bill's due day while the jar is short (more as dusk nears), angry once the bill is covered, stomping and sulking the morning after a bill is paid, shocked when his crow is chased off or the boss falls. He faints on the farm-won card. Reduced motion holds a still pose for each mood.
- Ads are never forced. Every reward (double a day, free coins, finish an upgrade, a late day) is a button the player can ignore, shown only while an ad is loaded, and granted only when AdMob reports the reward.
- A day is a transaction: an interrupted day replays from dawn (the engine saves its pre-day checkpoint).
- Side farms never touch the real farm. The daily farm and each duel turn take an engine `snapshot()`, switch saving off, seed the field (`setFieldSeed`: every crop, prop, crow and goat comes from its own seeded stream), drop skills and use the set loadout; at sunset the shell records the score and `restore()`s the snapshot, plan, tool, skills and sound. A daily try is counted when it starts, so quitting mid-day uses it up.
- The economy is tuned with bots (v0.12, `scripts/bots.mjs`): three players steer the sickle through the dev hook `window.__DBD_BOT` every engine step on real 15 s days (strong: computer-fast, never idle, steps around the goat; average: slower, retargets four times a second, pauses now and then; weak: slow, late, often lifts the finger), each with an on-track build for the farm (`expectedPower`). Bills ask for the average player's days plus half of the best of them (`DAY_SHAPE`, `AVG_POINTS`, `BILL_OVER` in `plan.ts`), a little more each bill (`BILL_CLIMB`), so the weak player can't pay even with a doubled day, the average one pays with one ad and the strong one needs none (Navid's target, 5 Nov). The rival's record is `RECORD_DAYS` average days in one. Crop toughness (`hpMul`) follows the expected build and crop value (`valMul`) keeps an on-track day in step with the bills; prices sit in `workshop.ts` and `tools.ts`. Fixed toughness curves were fragile (a strong build one-shot every crop and outran the bills, a weak one hit a wall), hence the square-root toughness.
- Android: the WebView keeps a text zoom of 100% (`MainActivity.lockTextSize`), because the phone's font-size setting otherwise enlarges every px font and pushes text out of the fixed cards.

## Verification and delivery

Run `npm ci`, `npm test`, `npm run build` and `npm run test:e2e` (five landscape sizes: the day loop, the farmhouse day card and every panel tab, tools, the boss farm with a test ad, settings and the volume sliders, a language switch that the whole game follows, the practice farm (each step waits for real cuts, the clock stays still) and Skip practice, the intro from Settings, a new-thing card holding the field, a chest upgraded by an ad to diamond, perks surviving a reload, the skill map, the Bling Barn, a daily farm that must leave the real farm untouched, a two-player duel, the first farm's carrots and chest, the payout mark on a replay, touch position, the power meter (a card at half full, then the power and the rush), a second finger doing nothing, crops away from the edges, and text staying inside its cards on a short screen). `npm run i18n:check` reports untranslated text. `npm run build:pc` plus `node scripts/inline-html.mjs dist-pc Due-by-Dusk.html` makes one self-contained HTML for playing on a computer (with labelled test ads).

Keep generated builds, dependencies, local SDK paths, signing material and test output out of Git.

Navid authorizes pushing tested updates to this repository. Commit and push coherent, verified changes; record any untested native/device behavior honestly. Publishing to an app store is a separate release step.

Sundown keeps the field music (`setMood('field')` for `farm` and `sundown`); only the farmhouse and the other menu screens bring the menu track. A perk card takes two taps (the first picks it out and shows "Take it"). A purchase that would leave less than tonight's bill asks on a card over a dimmed screen (`confirmVeil`); "Buy anyway" repeats the tap by finding its button again (the panel may have been redrawn). The Android WebView plays media without a gesture (`setMediaPlaybackRequiresUserGesture(false)` in MainActivity), and the app unlocks the music and the engine's sound at launch. Canada's field lies under snow (a white layer with furrow lines, snow in the crops' mounds and in the clods they push up).
